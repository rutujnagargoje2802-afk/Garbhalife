ABOUT GARBHALIFE

A Conscious Beginning For Motherhood

Guiding mothers towards a healthy, positive and mindful pregnancy journey through Ayurveda and Garbhasanskar.

MEET YOUR GUIDE

Dr. Prajakta
Khade-Gite

Dr. Prajakta Khade-Gite is an Ayurvedic doctor and Garbhasanskar consultant from Pune, dedicated to supporting women through a healthy, positive and mindful pregnancy journey.

Her approach brings together traditional Ayurvedic wisdom with practical guidance for today's mothers. Through personalized pregnancy care, yoga, meditation, music therapy and conscious parenting practices, she aims to create a nurturing environment for both mother and baby.

Through her initiative Garbhalife Garbhasanskar”, Dr. Prajakta works towards helping expecting mothers experience pregnancy as a meaningful journey of connection, awareness and preparation for motherhood.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Pregnancy Is More Than
A Physical Journey

Pregnancy is a beautiful phase of transformation. It is a time when a mother begins forming a deep connection with the child growing within her.

At Garbhalife, we believe that caring for the mother means caring for the beginning of a new life. Emotional wellbeing, positive thoughts, mindful lifestyle practices and meaningful bonding can all become part of this beautiful journey.
