About Dr. Prajakta

Dr. Prajakta Khade-Gite is an Ayurvedic doctor and Garbhasanskar consultant from Pune dedicated to guiding women through a mindful and healthy pregnancy journey. Through Suhrud Garbhasanskar, she combines Ayurveda, yoga, meditation, music therapy, and emotional wellness for holistic motherhood care.
